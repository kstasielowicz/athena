RIS

What is RIS?

Relative Index for Streetlifting

It compares your tested strength against your bodyweight, so the result is not just “how much you lift”, but how strong you are relative to your size.

Simple idea: RIS = your test total ÷ expected strength for your bodyweight × 100. In ATHENA, the expected strength number is produced by a bodyweight curve. Heavier athletes are expected to lift more, but not in a perfectly linear way. UPPER mode uses only pull-up + dip, while ALL-4 uses pull-up + dip + squat + muscle-up.

More information about RIS calculation and interpretation in the Relative Index for Streetlifting - 2025 Edition.

RIS levels

How to read your score

These bands are practical product levels, not medical or federation standards. Use them to track direction, not to judge one isolated test.

Foundation< 70

Baseline phase. Build technique, consistency and repeatable training weeks.

Developing70-84

Solid momentum. Strength is becoming visible relative to bodyweight.

Strong85-99

Good relative strength. Progression should become more structured.

Advanced100-114

High-performing range. Recovery, fatigue and programming details matter more.

Elite115+

Exceptional range. Small improvements require precise planning and testing standards.
